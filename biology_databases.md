# High-quality biology databases (for building and checking questions)

Use these to ground questions in real data and to check facts. If an answer depends on a database value, record the database version or date, because entries change.

## Sequences and genomes
- NCBI GenBank / RefSeq: nucleotide and curated reference sequences (ncbi.nlm.nih.gov)
- Ensembl: annotated vertebrate genomes, gene models, comparative genomics (ensembl.org)
- UCSC Genome Browser: genome annotation tracks, conservation (genome.ucsc.edu)
- ENA (European Nucleotide Archive) and DDBJ: the GenBank partners

## Proteins and structure
- UniProt (Swiss-Prot is the reviewed part): protein function, domains, variants (uniprot.org)
- PDB (RCSB / PDBe): experimental 3D structures (rcsb.org)
- AlphaFold DB: predicted structures (alphafold.ebi.ac.uk)
- InterPro / Pfam: protein families and domains (ebi.ac.uk/interpro)

## Variation and human genetics
- dbSNP: short variants
- gnomAD: population allele frequencies (gnomad.broadinstitute.org)
- ClinVar: clinical significance of variants
- OMIM: Mendelian disorders and genes (omim.org)
- 1000 Genomes / IGSR: population haplotypes

## Pathways, interactions and function
- KEGG: metabolic and signalling pathways (genome.jp/kegg)
- Reactome: curated human pathways (reactome.org)
- Gene Ontology: function annotations (geneontology.org)
- STRING and BioGRID: protein and genetic interactions
- BRENDA: enzyme kinetics (Km, kcat), conditions (brenda-enzymes.org)

## Expression
- GTEx: tissue-specific expression in humans (gtexportal.org)
- GEO and ArrayExpress: raw expression datasets
- Human Protein Atlas: protein expression and localisation (proteinatlas.org)

## Model organisms
- FlyBase (Drosophila), WormBase (C. elegans), SGD (yeast), TAIR (Arabidopsis), MGI (mouse), ZFIN (zebrafish), Xenbase (Xenopus)

## Evolution, ecology and diversity
- TimeTree: divergence times (timetree.org)
- Open Tree of Life: phylogeny
- GBIF: species occurrence records (gbif.org)
- NCBI Taxonomy

## Literature
- PubMed / PubMed Central: primary literature
- Europe PMC: full-text search

## Deep areas where models may slip (ideas for future question sets)
- Meiosis and chromatid tracking: automixis, unreduced gametes, tetrads (current weakness)
- Polyploid genetics: double reduction, tetrasomic segregation, triploid offspring
- Coalescent and gene genealogies: conditional coalescence times, sample structure
- Quantitative genetics: response to selection with linkage disequilibrium (Bulmer effect), heritability under inbreeding
- Enzyme kinetics: mixed inhibition, cooperative binding (Hill equations), steady-state assumptions
- Bioenergetics: proton motive force, P/O ratios with different shuttles
- Developmental genetics: maternal-effect and imprinting pedigrees
- Epidemiology and ecology: structured population models, R0 with several host types
