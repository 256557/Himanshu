# Probe batch 4: mixed biology, 5 questions

## Questions (type as written)

D1. A woman's brother and her maternal uncle both have hemophilia A. She has two unaffected sons. What is the probability that she is a carrier?
D2. A recessive allele has frequency 0.01. What is the probability that a child of first cousins is homozygous for it? Give the answer to six decimal places.
D3. 10 mL of 0.1 M NaOH is added to 50 mL of 0.1 M acetic acid (pKa 4.76). What is the pH?
D4. Urine flow is 1.2 mL/min. Urine PAH is 12 mg/mL, plasma PAH is 0.02 mg/mL, and the hematocrit is 45%. What is the renal blood flow?
D5. Oxygen consumption is 250 mL/min. Arterial O2 content is 19 mL/dL, pulmonary artery O2 content is 14 mL/dL, and the heart rate is 70/min. What is the stroke volume?

## Answer key (do not show to the model)

| # | Answer | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | 1/5 | Her mother is an obligate carrier (affected son and affected brother), so the prior is 1/2. Two unaffected sons: (1/2 x 1/4) / (1/2 x 1/4 + 1/2) = 1/5. Ignoring the sons gives 1/2. |
| D2 | 0.000719 | q^2 + F p q with F = 1/16: 0.0001 + 0.000619. Using only F q = 0.000625, or only q^2 = 0.0001, is wrong. |
| D3 | 4.16 | 1 mmol OH- turns 1 mmol HA into A-: A- = 1, HA = 4, pH = 4.76 + log(1/4). Using the starting acid (log 1/5) gives 4.06. |
| D4 | about 1309 mL/min | RPF = 12 x 1.2 / 0.02 = 720 mL/min, and RBF = RPF / (1 - Hct) = 720 / 0.55. Stopping at 720 (plasma flow), or dividing by 0.45, is wrong. |
| D5 | about 71 mL | Fick: CO = 250 / (5 mL/dL) = 50 dL/min = 5 L/min, and SV = 5000 / 70. Unit slips give 7.1 mL or 714 mL. |
