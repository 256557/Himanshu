## Q1

Step 1. Parameters. Mutation rate u per X in eggs and 1u in sperm; affected males have relative fitness f = 0.

Step 2. Equilibrium allele frequency in females. A daughter's maternal X carries the allele at frequency qf + u, and her paternal X at f(qf + u) + 1u. Setting qf equal to the average of the two gives qf = u[(1 + f) + k]/(1 - f) = 2u.

Step 3. Mother's prior carrier probability. Nothing is known about her parents, so she is a carrier with the population frequency C = 2qf = 4u.

Step 4. Prior odds given one affected son. The son is affected through a carrier mother with probability C/2 = 2u, or through a new mutation in her egg with probability u. The odds that she is a carrier are 2 : 1.

Step 5. Conditioning on 3 unaffected brothers. A carrier mother has this many unaffected sons with probability (1/2)^3 = 1/8. The posterior odds are 1/4 : 1, so P(mother carrier) = 1/5.

Step 6. The daughter and her son. The daughter is a carrier with probability 1/10, and her first son is affected with probability 1/10 x 1/2 = 1/20 = 0.0500.
