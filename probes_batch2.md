# Probe batch 2: short questions across biology

## Questions (type as written)

B1. How many high-energy phosphate bonds (ATP plus GTP) are spent to make one glucose from two pyruvate by gluconeogenesis?
B2. A PCR starts with one double-stranded DNA molecule. After 3 cycles, how many double-stranded molecules have exactly the target length?
B3. A person breathes 12 times per minute with a 500 mL tidal volume and has 150 mL of anatomical dead space. They switch to 24 breaths per minute at 250 mL. What is the new alveolar ventilation, in L/min?
B4. How many chromatids are in one human cell at metaphase II?
B5. Fifty fish are marked and released, but 20% lose their marks before resampling. A second catch of 40 fish has 8 marked. Estimate the population size.
B6. A population has 100 females and 100 males. What is the fixation probability of a new neutral mitochondrial mutation?
B7. A competitive inhibitor is present at a concentration equal to its Ki, and the substrate is at its Km. What fraction of Vmax is the reaction rate?
B8. At 37 C, free calcium is 0.0001 mM inside a cell and 2 mM outside. What is the calcium equilibrium potential, in mV?

## Answer key (do not show to the model)

| # | Answer | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | 6 (4 ATP + 2 GTP), plus 2 NADH | Counting only the 4 ATP, or answering 2 (glycolysis in reverse). |
| B2 | 2 | 2^n - 2n = 8 - 6 = 2. Answering 8 counts all copies, including the longer ones. |
| B3 | 2.4 L/min (was 4.2) | Minute ventilation stays at 6 L/min, but each breath now ventilates only 100 mL of alveolar air. |
| B4 | 46 | 23 chromosomes x 2 chromatids. Common errors are 23 or 92. |
| B5 | 200 | Only 40 fish carry marks, so N = 40 x 40 / 8. Naive 50 x 40 / 8 = 250. |
| B6 | 1/100 | Mitochondria are haploid and inherited only through females. The nuclear answer is 1/400. |
| B7 | 1/3 | v = S / (Km(1 + I/Ki) + S) = 1 / (2 + 1). The common slip is 1/4. |
| B8 | about +132 mV | z = 2 halves the slope: 30.8 x log10(20000). Using z = 1 gives about +265. |
