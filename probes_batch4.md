# Probe batch 4: biology only, 5 questions

## Questions (type as written)

D1. A woman's brother and her maternal uncle both have hemophilia A. She has two unaffected sons. What is the probability that she is a carrier?
D2. A recessive allele has frequency 0.01. What is the probability that a child of first cousins is homozygous for it? Give the answer to six decimal places.
D3. After splicing, an mRNA has a coding sequence of 900 nucleotides, from the start codon through the stop codon. How many amino acids does the protein have?
D4. A population grows logistically with r = 0.2 per day and K = 1000. What is the largest number of individuals it can add per day?
D5. In a cell with 2n = 8, one chromosome pair fails to separate in meiosis I. What fraction of the gametes are n + 1?

## Answer key (do not show to the model)

| # | Answer | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | 1/5 | Her mother is an obligate carrier (affected son and affected brother), so the prior is 1/2. Two unaffected sons: (1/2 x 1/4) / (1/2 x 1/4 + 1/2) = 1/5. Ignoring the sons gives 1/2. |
| D2 | 0.000719 | q^2 + F p q with F = 1/16: 0.0001 + 0.000619. Using only F q = 0.000625, or only q^2 = 0.0001, is wrong. |
| D3 | 299 | 900 / 3 = 300 codons, but the stop codon codes no amino acid. Common error: 300. |
| D4 | 50 per day | Growth rN(1 - N/K) peaks at N = K/2 = 500, giving rK/4 = 0.2 x 1000 / 4. Common errors: 200 (rK) or 100. |
| D5 | 1/2 | Nondisjunction in meiosis I gives 2 n+1 and 2 n-1 gametes. 1/4 is the meiosis II answer. |
